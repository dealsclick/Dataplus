DataPlus Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Operator: LINQ USA CORP

Private order-management system. DataPlus is a private, internal business application used by authorized LINQ USA CORP personnel to manage marketplace products, inventory, orders, fulfillment, shipping, returns, accounting support, and related compliance. It is not a consumer service, advertising platform, or data broker.

1. Scope and processing role

This policy explains how LINQ USA CORP processes information through DataPlus when an authorized seller connects a marketplace shop, including a Temu shop. DataPlus acts only on behalf of the authorized seller and connected marketplace for the operational purposes described in this policy.

DataPlus accesses marketplace information only after the seller authorizes the connection. Access is limited to the permissions approved for that shop and can be revoked by disconnecting the shop or revoking authorization in the marketplace.

2. Information processed

Depending on the authorized features and the information supplied by the marketplace, DataPlus may process:

  • Seller and shop information: shop name, shop ID, account ID, region, authorization status, API permissions, and token metadata.
  • Order information: marketplace order number, order status, dates, items, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, shipping charges, refunds, cancellations, and transaction references.
  • Buyer fulfillment information: recipient name, delivery address, telephone number, masked or marketplace-provided email address, and delivery instructions.
  • Product information: SKU, merchant SKU, product identifiers, title, description, images, dimensions, inventory, pricing, and listing status.
  • Shipping and return information: package details, carrier, service, label, tracking number, shipment status, return status, and supporting operational records.
  • Operational and security information: authorized user, action time, job status, API result, error details, IP address, and security or audit events.

DataPlus is not intended to collect payment-card numbers, bank credentials, government identification, precise location history, advertising profiles, or marketplace account passwords from buyers.

3. Sources of information

Information is received from connected marketplace APIs, the authorized seller and its personnel, approved catalog or supplier sources, and carriers or logistics providers used to complete an order. DataPlus does not obtain buyer information from data brokers.

4. How information is used

DataPlus uses information only to provide private order-management and related operational services, including:

  • importing, displaying, reviewing, and fulfilling authorized marketplace orders;
  • matching products, SKUs, listings, inventory, and warehouse stock;
  • preparing shipping rates, labels, tracking updates, returns, and customer-service responses;
  • publishing and maintaining products for an authorized shop;
  • reconciling sales, fees, costs, refunds, inventory movements, and accounting evidence;
  • preventing duplicate processing, troubleshooting failures, maintaining security, and complying with marketplace or legal requirements.

DataPlus does not sell personal information, use buyer information for behavioral advertising, create unrelated consumer profiles, or use marketplace personal data for purposes outside the authorized seller relationship.

5. Disclosure and subprocessors

Information is disclosed only when needed to operate the authorized service, comply with law, protect the service, or complete an order.

6. Security

DataPlus uses administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability. These safeguards include restricted authenticated access, role-based permissions, company and shop separation, HTTPS/TLS for external traffic, database and network access controls, audit records, controlled deployments, backups, and security monitoring.

Marketplace application secrets, access tokens, and refresh tokens are encrypted separately using AES-256-GCM and are not displayed after storage. Production credentials are kept outside source code. Access is limited to personnel who need the information for authorized operations.

No security measure is absolute. LINQ USA CORP reviews access, dependencies, changes, and incidents and takes corrective action when a risk is identified.

7. Retention, service completion, and deletion

Personal data is retained only while needed to provide the authorized order-management service, protect the service, resolve disputes, or satisfy applicable legal, tax, accounting, and audit obligations.

When services for a Temu shop are completed, or when Temu provides a valid deletion instruction, LINQ USA CORP will delete or de-identify Temu personal data within 30 days unless retention is required by applicable law. Information retained under a legal exception is restricted to that compliance purpose, is not used for unrelated operations or marketing, and is deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

Shop offboarding includes revoking or removing active credentials, stopping new synchronization, identifying retained copies, deleting or de-identifying personal data, addressing recoverable backups under the backup lifecycle, and documenting completion. An authorized administrator records any legal exception and its applicable retention period.

8. Privacy requests and marketplace instructions

LINQ USA CORP assists Temu and other connected marketplaces with verified requests to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete personal data. A buyer may submit a request through the marketplace where the order was placed or contact LINQ USA CORP using the address below.

Requests are verified before information is disclosed or changed. DataPlus personnel search the relevant shop, order, shipment, return, customer, and audit records; apply the approved action; and document completion. The target response period is 30 days unless a shorter period is required by the marketplace or applicable law.

9. International processing

DataPlus production infrastructure is located in the United States. Marketplace APIs, carriers, or authorized service providers may process information in other jurisdictions. Any cross-border transfer must follow applicable law and the connected marketplace's requirements.

10. Security incidents

Suspected unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure is investigated and contained promptly. When an incident affects Temu data, LINQ USA CORP will notify Temu within 24 hours after detection and will provide available information, remediation progress, and reasonable assistance.

11. Governance

LINQ USA CORP maintains an internal personal-data protection policy covering access, retention, deletion, incident response, subprocessors, change management, training, and audit evidence. The policy is reviewed at least annually and after material changes or security incidents.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in DataPlus, marketplace requirements, subprocessors, or applicable law. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes are reviewed before publication.
